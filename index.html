Who I am

Raw data is cheap.
Trustworthy data isn't.

I've spent seven years in analytics across water utilities, building research and medical devices. Most of that work has been turning messy operational data into something a business can act on.

02

Customer Churn Prediction

A churn model for a telecom company, built on IBM's public Telco sample dataset (7,032 California customers after cleaning). Month-to-month customers churned at 42.7%, against 2.8% on two-year contracts. A Random Forest caught 49% of churners at the default 0.5 threshold; lowering the threshold to 0.3 raised recall to 76%, at a cost in precision. Churn Score and Churn Reason were dropped before training, since both are only known after a customer leaves. Results are summarised in a Tableau dashboard.
